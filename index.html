Before You Go

Everything you need before wheels hit the road — rules, tools, and hard-won warnings from the trail.

Golden Rules

  • Drive left — always. NZ roads are narrow; allow extra following distance.
  • Freedom camping only where signage allows. Use DOC & council apps; pay where required.
  • Dump stations every 2–3 days minimum. Never empty grey/black water in streams.
  • Fuel — fill before remote stretches (West Coast, Milford, Catlins). Diesel is widely available.
  • Speed limits — 100 km/h open road, 50 urban. Slow for one-lane bridges and gravel.
  • Insurance — understand excess, windscreen, and underbody coverage for gravel roads.
  • Leave No Trace — pack out all rubbish; respect Māori sacred sites and private land.

Essential Apps

  • CamperMate — free camps, dump stations, WiFi, fuel.
  • Rankers Camping NZ — paid & DOC camps with reviews.
  • MetService — mountain & road weather; critical for passes.
  • AA Roadwatch / Waze — closures, slips, winter chains.
  • Google Maps offline — download South Island regions before departure.
  • BookMyVehicle / ParkPass — DOC huts & Great Walks where applicable.
  • Gaspy — cheapest fuel near you.

Critical Warnings

  • Sandflies — Fiordland & West Coast. Repellent + covered ankles at dusk.
  • Weather swings — four seasons in a day; carry layers & rain gear always.
  • Milford Highway (94) — book early; long day; no fuel at Milford Sound.
  • One-lane bridges — blue arrow = you yield; slow before crests.
  • Gravel roads — reduce speed; stone chips void some windscreen policies.
  • Kea birds — at Arthur's Pass & alpine carparks — they destroy rubber seals.
  • Phone signal — patchy on West Coast & Catlins; tell someone your route.

Your 21-Day Itinerary

A clockwise South Island loop from Christchurch: harbours, turquoise lakes, alpine passes, Queenstown adrenaline, Fiordland rainforests, the Catlins coast, wildlife highways, and glacier country — returning via Punakaiki's pancake rocks.

Day 01

Christchurch — Arrival & Campervan Handover

Airport → Depot: 15 min City loop: 30 min

Collect your campervan from the airport or city depot. Complete the walk-through: water, gas, electrics, awning, and emergency kit. Stock groceries at Countdown Moorhouse or New World Durham Street. Evening stroll the Avon River, Christchurch Botanic Gardens, and the Re:START / Riverside precinct.

Stay: Spencer Beach Holiday Park or Lyttelton Harbour for a harbour sunset.

Insider Tip: Book a late-morning pickup so you can shop and still reach your first camp before dark — NZ sunset times vary wildly by season.

Day 02

Christchurch → Akaroa (Banks Peninsula)

Drive: 1 hr 15 min Summit Road optional: +45 min

Cross the crater rim into French-flavoured Akaroa. Harbour dolphin cruises (Hector's dolphins), waterfront cafés, and the Giant's House stairs. Optional Summit Road scenic detour for volcano views.

Stay: Akaroa Top 10 or Garden Street freedom-camping-approved areas (check CamperMate).

Insider Tip: The Summit Road is unsealed in sections — confirm your rental allows gravel before committing.

Day 03

Akaroa → Lake Tekapo

Drive: 3 hr

Return via Christchurch or take inland Route 72 for Canterbury high-country scenery. Arrive at turquoise Lake Tekapo — Church of the Good Shepherd, bronze sheepdog memorial, and Mount John Observatory (book stargazing or daytime café visit).

Stay: Lake Tekapo Motels & Holiday Park or lakeside DOC sites nearby.

Insider Tip: Mount John is a dark-sky reserve — go after moonset for Milky Way photos; bring a headlamp with red light mode.

Day 04

Lake Tekapo → Aoraki / Mount Cook

Drive: 1 hr 5 min

Drive the shore of Lake Pukaki to New Zealand's highest peak. Walk Hooker Valley Track (3–4 hrs return, flat) to the glacier lake with icebergs. Visit Sir Edmund Hillary Alpine Centre if weather closes trails.

Stay: White Horse Hill Campground (DOC) or Mount Cook Village lodges.

Insider Tip: Start Hooker Valley before 9am — coach groups arrive mid-morning and the carpark fills fast in peak season.

Day 05

Mount Cook → Omarama → Wanaka

Drive: 2 hr 30 min Lindis Pass: 1 hr

Optional sunrise at Tasman Lake. Cross Lindis Pass golden tussock into Central Otago. Stop Omarama for glider capital views and clay cliffs. Arrive Wanaka — lakefront, #ThatWanakaTree, and relaxed dining on Ardmore Street.

Stay: Wanaka Lakeview Holiday Park or Albert Town camp near outlet.

Insider Tip: Clay Cliffs are on private land — small honesty-box fee; wear closed shoes, it's dusty and steep.

Day 06

Wanaka — Rest & Explore Day

Local drives: under 45 min

No major relocation — recharge. Options: Roys Peak (full day, book parking), Isthmus Peak, Puzzling World, kayak to Ruby Island, or bike the lake trail. Laundry and supermarket catch-up day.

Stay: Same as Day 5 — enjoy a second night without packing.

Insider Tip: Roys Peak carpark now requires online booking in summer — the DOC site sells out weeks ahead.

Day 07

Wanaka → Queenstown via Cardrona

Drive: 1 hr 15 min Crown Range: 45 min

Scenic route over Crown Range (NZ's highest sealed pass) or safer Crown Range alternative via Cromwell. Stop Cardrona Hotel & bra fence, historic gold town Arrowtown, then descend into Queenstown — gondola, lakefront, and Fergburger pilgrimage.

Stay: Queenstown Lakeview Holiday Park or DOC Moke Lake (4WD/mountain bike access check).

Insider Tip: Crown Range closes in heavy snow — check NZTA before leaving Wanaka; carry chains Nov–Sep if permitted.

Day 08

Queenstown — Adventure & Lake Day

Local: 20 min max

Pick your thrill: Skyline gondola+luge, TSS Earnslaw steam cruise to Walter Peak, jet boat Shotover, bungy Nevis or Kawarau, or Glenorchy day-trip to Paradise filming country. Wine lovers: Gibbston Valley cellar doors 20 min away.

Stay: Queenstown — second night to avoid packing daily.

Insider Tip: Book bungy/nevis time slots online — walk-in queues can waste half a day in January.

Day 09

Queenstown → Milford Sound → Te Anau

Qtn → Milford: 4 hr Milford → Te Anau: 2 hr

Epic long day — depart before dawn. Mirror Lakes, Homer Tunnel, Stirling Falls cruise (pre-book). No fuel at Milford; fill at Te Anau morning or Queenstown night before. Evening recovery in Te Anau — glowworm caves optional night tour.

Stay: Te Anau Lakeview Kiwi Holiday Park & Motels.

Insider Tip: Homer Tunnel one-way lights mean delays — build 30–45 min buffer; coach convoys peak 10am–2pm.

Day 10

Te Anau — Fiordland Recovery Day

Kepler Track start: 15 min Doubtful Sound: full day

Gentle day after Milford. Lake Te Anau cruise, visit DOC Fiordland visitor centre, short Kepler day-hike to Control Gates, or book Doubtful Sound (less crowded than Milford). Sandfly repellent mandatory.

Stay: Te Anau — second night.

Insider Tip: Doubtful Sound tours leave from Manapouri — park camper at Te Anau, take shuttle; don't miss the boat connection.

Day 11

Te Anau → Invercargill → Bluff

Drive: 2 hr 15 min

Head south to the world's southernmost mainland point. Invercargill classic car museums and Queens Park. Bluff Hill lookout, Stirling Point signpost (Stewart Island ferry), and famous Bluff oysters in season (Mar–Aug).

Stay: Bluff Camping Ground or Invercargill TOP 10.

Insider Tip: Foveaux Strait weather cancels Stewart Island ferries often — only add Rakiura if you have buffer days.

Day 12

Bluff → Curio Bay → The Catlins Coast

Drive: 1 hr 30 min Coastal stops: flexible

Drive the Southern Scenic Route. Petrified forest at Curio Bay (yellow-eyed penguins at dusk), Slope Point southernmost road, Nugget Point lighthouse, waterfalls: Purakaunui & McLean Falls. Remote, lush, and uncrowded.

Stay: Papatowai Camping Ground or Waikawa Museum camp.

Insider Tip: Never flash photos at penguins — use red-filter torches only and keep 10 m distance; rangers fine offenders.

Day 13

The Catlins → Dunedin

Drive: 2 hr

Finish Catlins highlights, then north to Edinburgh of the South. Baldwin Street (steepest), Otago Peninsula for albatross colony (Taiaroa Head), Larnach Castle, and student-city craft beer at Emerson's or New New New.

Stay: Dunedin Holiday Park or DOC sites on peninsula (book albatross tour).

Insider Tip: Peninsula road is narrow — start albatross tour early; camper parking at Taiaroa is tight, consider shuttle.

Day 14

Dunedin → Moeraki Boulders → Oamaru

Drive: 1 hr 45 min Low tide essential

Coastal SH1. Time Moeraki spherical boulders at low tide — café above the beach. Continue to Oamaru Victorian precinct, steampunk playground, blue penguin evening parade (protected viewing stands).

Stay: Oamaru Top 10 or Harbour Street area camps.

Insider Tip: Check tide tables the night before — high tide reduces boulders to submerged bumps.

Day 15

Oamaru → Timaru → Geraldine

Drive: 2 hr

Inland via pleasant South Canterbury farmland. Timaru Caroline Bay promenade. Geraldine — Barker's fruit wines & jam shop, boarding town charm, and gateway to Peel Forest day-walks if time allows.

Stay: Geraldine TOP 10 Holiday Park.

Insider Tip: Barker's tasting room closes early — stop before 4:30pm, not after a long drive-in at dusk.

Day 16

Geraldine → Kaikōura

Drive: 2 hr 45 min

Cross Canterbury to the Pacific coast. Kaikōura Peninsula Walkway — seals year-round, seabirds, snow mountains behind ocean. Pre-book whale-watching flight or boat for tomorrow. Seafood BBQ vans on the waterfront.

Stay: Kaikōura TOP 10 Holiday Park (peninsula views).

Insider Tip: Seal pups look cuddly but adults bite — stay 10 m; never stand between seal and the sea.

Day 17

Kaikōura — Whale Watch & Coastal Day

Local: under 15 min

Morning whale encounter (sperm whales resident year-round; tours refund if no sighting policy varies). Afternoon: lavender farm, Maori cultural tours, or peninsula kayak with fur seals. Relax — you've earned a slower day.

Stay: Kaikōura — second night.

Insider Tip: Seasick on boats? Fixed-wing whale flights from the airfield cost more but no swell — book weather-dependent slots first thing.

Day 18

Kaikōura → Hanmer Springs

Drive: 2 hr

Inland through Waipara wine country (Riesling & Pinot Noir tastings). Arrive alpine Hanmer Springs — thermal pools complex, forest walks, and Conical Hill lookout. Perfect muscle recovery after 17 driving days.

Stay: Hanmer Springs TOP 10 or Heritage Hanmer motor camp.

Insider Tip: Thermal pools get packed 11am–3pm — evening soak under stars is quieter and photogenic.

Day 19

Hanmer Springs → Arthur's Pass → Hokitika

Drive: 3 hr 30 min Arthur's Pass: 1 hr

Cross the Southern Alps via Arthur's Pass — Devil's Punchbowl waterfall walk, kea spotting at the village, Otira Viaduct viewpoints. Descend to West Coast rainforest and greenstone town Hokitika — beach driftwood art, glowworm dell dusk walk.

Stay: Hokitika TOP 10 or Lake Kaniere campsite.

Insider Tip: Cover windscreen wipers and door rubber at Arthur's Pass — kea destruction is real and rental claims hurt.

Day 20

Hokitika → Franz Josef & Fox Glaciers

Drive: 1 hr 30 min

South along wild coastline. Franz Josef Glacier Valley walk (terminal face viewpoint — glacier retreating, check DOC status). Fox Glacier Te Moeka walk. Optional heli-hike if budget allows. Lake Matheson mirror reflection at dawn/dusk if weather clear.

Stay: Franz Josef TOP 10 or DOC Franz Josef campsite.

Insider Tip: Lake Matheson is calmest at sunrise — set alarm once on this trip; cloud-free mornings are rare but legendary.

Day 21

Franz Josef → Punakaiki → Christchurch (Loop Complete)

Franz → Punakaiki: 2 hr Punakaiki → CHC: 4 hr

Final coastal push — Pancake Rocks & blowholes at Punakaiki (high tide best for blowholes). Cross the island via Arthur's Pass or Lewis Pass depending on weather. Return campervan Christchurch — allow 2 hrs cleaning & dump station before depot deadline. Celebrate with Sumner beach fish & chips.

Finish: Christchurch Airport or city depot drop-off — book afternoon flight buffer.

Insider Tip: Photograph fuel gauge and odometer at return; keep all toll and ferry receipts until deposit refund clears — usually 7–14 days.

Safe travels — see you on the road.
